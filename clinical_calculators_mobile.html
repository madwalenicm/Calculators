Adrenaline infusion calculator

Preparation and pump-rate arithmetic for a clinician-selected dose and final concentration.

Prototype — not validated for clinical use. Adrenaline is a high-alert medication. Confirm the indication, prescribed dose, local preparation protocol, stock ampoule concentration, diluent, vascular access, infusion pump and independent double-check before administration. This is for continuous infusions, not resuscitation bolus dosing.

Calculation check

Once values are entered, this panel displays: drug amount, volume withdrawn from stock, final concentration, infusion pump rate and a reverse dose check.

Formulas

Final concentration (µg/mL) = total adrenaline (mg) × 1000 ÷ final volume (mL).

Pump rate (mL/h) = dose (µg/kg/min) × weight (kg) × 60 ÷ final concentration (µg/mL).

“Final total volume” means the prepared syringe/bag volume including the adrenaline solution, not diluent volume added on top. Do not mix based solely on this calculator.
Arithmetic consistent with the South African NDoH Paediatric Hospital-Level STGs & EML, Paediatric Intensive Care, 2023, concentration/rate formula. The app intentionally does not select a dose or prescribe a dilution. View reference.
